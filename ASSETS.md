# Assets

`frontend/assets/barbershop.jpg` was downloaded from the Unsplash image endpoint:
https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1503951914875-452162b0f3f1

It is used only as sample barbershop imagery. The sample business, people, appointments, and contact details are fictional and do not identify the people or business in the photograph.

The LocalLaunch mark, favicon, and interface icons are SVG artwork included in this repository. Fonts use the device's system sans-serif and Georgia serif; no external font service is called.
